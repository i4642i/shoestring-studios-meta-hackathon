ABOUT — WHY WE BUILD THIS

MOTIVATION

Built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon 2026 (Indie track — Build for Your World): a working AI video crew, assembled in days on Meta models, that turns dense knowledge into videos worth publishing.

THE PROBLEM

Dense papers and technical documents are hard to share as video. NotebookLM-class auto-summarizers drop key points, skip what matters, and their videos are not good enough to publish. A busy domain expert who wants to become a solo knowledge creator cannot afford a video team — so knowledge worth sharing never ships.

THE ANSWER

Shoestring Studios is a full AI video crew on a shoestring budget: brief-driven explainer videos, gated by producer QA (accuracy + coverage, revise until pass), built in the open like a cooking show. Publish-grade, not summary-grade.

OPEN SOURCE

Built on free and open-source software only (no paid packs or plans). Full license texts ship with the code where noted.
