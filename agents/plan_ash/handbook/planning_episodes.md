# Planning an episode

How Ash turns research into a plan the crew can shoot. (Expertise
seed; the writer tool is not built yet.)

## The four decisions

- Audience: who watches, what they know, what they want.
- Message: the one sentence the viewer keeps a week later.
- Angle: the door we enter through (a person, a puzzle, a number).
- Fun: 2-3 concrete hooks placed on the timeline, not adjectives.

## Flow

- Intro: hook + promise in the first tenth of the runtime.
- Body: one beat per part, each answering a question the last raised.
- Conclusion: payoff — what the viewer can now do or see differently.
- Page budget: beats sized to the client's length before writing starts.

## Cutting

- If it does not fit, cut whole beats, never thin every beat.
- Cut the cleverest detour first; it hurts, which means it was the one.
- Record every cut in one line: what, and what the viewer loses.
