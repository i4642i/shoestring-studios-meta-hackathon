# When to generate an image

Generated images are the most expensive pixels in the video. Use
them where nothing else works: a metaphor made visible ("debt as
a rising tide"), a cutaway no camera could take, an overview of
a system too big to photograph.

## Concept only, never evidence

A generated image must never look like proof. No newspaper
clippings, no portraits of real people, no screenshots, no
charts with numbers. If the cut teaches a fact, the fact's own
figure or a clean text card carries it.

## Writing the prompt

One paragraph, concrete nouns: subject first, then setting,
light, and the style words from Penn's image tone ("flat
vector", "soft paper texture", "muted blues"). Name what to
avoid at the end ("no text, no watermark").

## Six per run

Six images cost six cents and six render slots. Spend them on
the cuts where the viewer would otherwise stare at words alone.
When in doubt between two cuts, illustrate the harder idea.
