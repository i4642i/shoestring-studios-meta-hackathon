# Writing narration

How Wren turns checked pages into lines worth hearing. (Expertise
seed; the writer tool is not built yet.)

## Line craft

- One breath per sentence. Read every line aloud in your head.
- Concrete nouns, active verbs. "The guild struck" beats "a strike
  was undertaken by the guild".
- New terms get a same-sentence gloss, or they wait for their page.

## Section voices

- Intro: invite and promise. Short lines, open questions, no spoilers.
- Body: teach steadily. One idea per line, facts with their weight.
- Outro: land it. The message, the payoff, a last image to keep.

## Evidence

- Every fact and number carries a source ref from the checked pages.
- If a line has no evidence, rewrite it until it does — or cut it.
- Evidence never leaks into the spoken line; it rides alongside.
