# Planning one slide per cut

A slide plan is a promise Remy can build without asking
questions. Name the visual, place the text, fix the time.

## Two materials, at most

The viewer reads one thing at a time. A photo plus a caption
line; a chart plus its title; a figure plus a highlight. Two
materials is the ceiling, one is the norm, and a text card —
Penn's lines on the guide background — is always allowed.

## Look before you plan

Open the candidate file. Read small text in it; check faces,
logos, and watermarks; confirm the license allows our use. A
candidate you cannot open is a candidate you cannot plan.

## Text carries the cut

On-screen text repeats the cut's one takeaway, never the whole
narration. Three lines at most, short enough to read twice in
the cut's seconds. When the narration is dense, the text shows
the anchor (name, number, term) and lets the voice explain.
