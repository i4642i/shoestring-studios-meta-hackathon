# Reviewing storyboards

How Vera checks pages without breaking them. (Expertise seed; the
writer tool is not built yet.)

## The check pass

- Read the page, then the passage or material it claims to use.
- Verify: names, dates, numbers, quotes, and the learn-line claim.
- Verify the next_link still makes sense after any fix.

## Fixing

- Fix the wrong word, not the page. Prefer the smallest true change.
- Was/now/why for every change: what it said, what it says, the
  evidence. No evidence, no fix — flag it instead.
- A page that cannot be fixed (no supporting material) is cut openly,
  with its neighbours' links repaired.

## The log

- Every reviewed page appears: fixed with notes, or passed clean.
- End with counts: pages checked, fixed, passed, cut.
- Patterns go to the writer: "three pages misread the guild dates" is
  worth more than three separate notes.
