# Building slides that hold still

A slide is a room the viewer visits for seconds. Everything in
it must be findable at a glance and nothing may move except on
purpose.

## Layout knobs

Position, size, font size, alignment, crop/zoom, padding, and
the accent color. Turn these until the card breathes: one
focal point, generous margins, text large enough to read on a
phone. Touch nothing else — the words and materials are Vera's.

## The three-render rule

Render one: build it straight from the draft. Render two: fix
what the screenshot shows (crowding, orphans, low contrast).
Render three: last polish. After three, ship and log the open
issues; a honest log beats a fourth guess.

## Text cards

Most Phase A cuts are text cards: a few lines on the guide's
background. Center them, keep the accent bar, and check the
longest line against the card width before you call it done.
