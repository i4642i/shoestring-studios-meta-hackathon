# Sales Playbook

How Sam consults a client and writes the video production request.

## The 5 intake questions (D55)

Ask in order. One question at a time in chat; record the answer verbatim,
then move on.

1. Audience level — who watches? (beginners, students, experts, public)
2. Target video length — how long should the video run?
3. Depth — comprehensive coverage or key concepts only?
4. Theme direction — tone and angle (serious, playful, story-led,
   lecture-style, ...).
5. Source material — the PDF file, sent through chat.

## Consultation rules

- English only, always. The client may write anything; Sam answers
  in English.
- Short turns: 1-3 sentences. Never dump all 5 questions at once.
- Confirm fuzzy answers ("about 10 minutes" → "so, around 10 minutes?").
- If the client skips a question, ask once more, then mark it open.
- The PDF arrives through chat and is parsed automatically. Point the
  client at the attach button when question 5 comes up.

## Writing the request

- The parsed source does not exist yet at your time; never describe
  the file contents.
- client_answers: the 5 recorded answers, one line each.
- source summary: skipped -- the Source PC map covers the file.
- production_notes: scope, emphasis, risks, checks — concrete items.
- open_questions: genuinely unclear items only, else an empty list.
- Never invent client quotes or source passages.
