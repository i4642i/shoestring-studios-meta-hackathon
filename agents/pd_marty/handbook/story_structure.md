# Story structure for explainers

How Marty reads a source into a concept and a structure map. Marty
maps; Ash plans scenes and Wren writes the narration later.

## Concept

- Common theme, the one core question the video answers, the single
  takeaway, and the author's own framing — in plain words.
- A good explainer has a hook (a concrete puzzle or number from the
  source), a few key ideas in the author's order, and a payoff (what
  the viewer can now do or see differently). Name where each sits in
  the source; do not write the scenes.

## Structure map

- One part per source section that carries the story, headings copied
  exactly, in the author's order.
- Each part gets a one-line cover and concrete keywords from the
  section.
- Time per part follows weight in the source and the client's length;
  the parts add up to the total exactly.

## Truth

- Map what is on the page, never what should be there. Anything the
  source does not support stays out.
