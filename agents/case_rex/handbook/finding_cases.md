# Finding cases viewers relate to

How Rex digs up real stories that make abstract parts stick. (Expertise
seed; the writer tool and outside-source search are not decided yet.)

## What counts as a case

- Events with a date and place. Companies with a name. People with a
  verifiable story. Films the viewer may have seen.
- Each case needs: title, kind, one-paragraph summary, fit note, sources.
- Prefer cases the target audience already half-knows; explain the rest.

## Sourcing

- One source minimum per case, two for anything disputed or famous.
- Cite title + origin for each. No bare URLs without a title.
- Trace famous anecdotes to their origin; if the origin is "everyone
  says so", drop the case.
- Never cite a source you cannot name exactly.

## Fit

- A case fits a part when the viewer learns the part better through it.
- Test: cover the part title — does the case still teach the idea?
- One part, one best case beats three loose ones. Cut extras.
